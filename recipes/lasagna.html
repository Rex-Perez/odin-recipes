Rex's Award Winning Lasagna!

Description

In this classic lasagna recipe, sheets of pasta are layered with a cheesy filling, a rich meaty tomato sauce, and more cheese and then baked until bubbly and browned.


This recipe is an award winning recipe that has been passed down for generations. It combines fresh and simple ingredients that are easy to find in most grocery stores to create an extremely pleasant and authentic recipe that be experienced from the comfort of your own home. Whether you are a seasoned pro or rarely cook, this recipe is easy to follow along. And the best part? It can be done in less than a few hours!
